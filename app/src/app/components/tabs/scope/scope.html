1 hop finds domains that shared a certificate with one of yours. Each further hop searches the domains just found, so a group structure is uncovered in full — but a single wrong match becomes the starting point for the next round, and mistakes compound.

{{ maxSans() }} names

A certificate naming a few domains together is a statement of ownership. One naming dozens is usually a hosting provider bundling unrelated customers. This is the setting to be most careful with: raising it does not simply find more, it weakens the reasoning behind every result, and can pull in strangers' domains.

{{ budget() }}

A safety limit on how much one search will do. Raising it costs only time and queries to the public log services; the search stops at the limit and tells you it did, so a low value never hides anything silently.

Why security leaders choose Trawl

Close the blind spots your internal tools never see

Internal scanners only check systems someone remembered to register. Meanwhile, marketing launches a subdomain, a firewall rule is widened temporarily, an email policy quietly weakens, or a developer commits a key to a public repository. Trawl closes that gap continuously.

1. Outside-in Visibility

See your real external footprint

Continuously discover internet-facing domains, services, and forgotten assets the same way an adversary does, without installing agents or handing out internal credentials.

2. Prioritisation You Can Defend

Rank by real exploitation, not noise

Focus engineering capacity on what is actively exploited in the wild and most likely to be targeted next. Every priority order is transparent and reproducible, never an opaque vendor score.

3. Regression Detection

Spot when something stops being fine

Most tools flood teams with "new" findings while missing quiet drift. Trawl compares every check against the last so a weakened email policy, TLS downgrade, or newly exposed database raises an immediate flag.

4. Brand & Credential Protection

Stop spoofing and leaked keys early

Validate anti-spoofing email controls across every domain and catch leaked credentials across the full history of your public repositories before someone else finds them.

5. Board-to-Engineer Clarity

Plain-English briefings and full history

Every finding explains what was found, why it matters, how it could be abused, and how to confirm the fix, backed by an immutable timeline showing when it appeared, changed, or was resolved.

6. Sovereign & Zero-Friction

Your attack surface stays yours

No third-party SaaS holds a map of your vulnerabilities. Deploy on a single workstation for an immediate review or run continuously inside your own environment at zero licensing cost.

Built for executive trust

"We did not look" must never look like "everything is fine"

Most security dashboards hide blind spots behind a reassuring green score. Trawl separates what was verified from what was missing, skipped, or inconclusive, so leadership always knows the real boundaries of assurance.

Verified

The control was checked and confirmed to be properly in place.

Missing

The check completed and confirmed the protection is absent - real work for the owning team.

Not checked

Excluded by policy or scope. Lowers reported coverage rather than counting as a pass.

Inconclusive

Attempted, but a timeout or network issue prevented an answer. Flagged as a gap, never as healthy.
